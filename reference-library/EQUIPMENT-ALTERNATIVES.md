# Equipment alternatives for workout selection

These groups keep sensible equipment choices discoverable. A workout chooses a member variation; each retains its own grip, contacts, configuration and evidence. They do not replace equipment inside an animation or assert equal loads.

## goblet-squat

A workout slot seeking a two-hand, chest-loaded squat can offer these researched equipment choices.

- [Dumbbell goblet squat, upper-head support](exercises/squat/dumbbell-goblet-head-support.md)
- [Kettlebell goblet squat, horns](exercises/squat/kettlebell-goblet-squat.md)

Dumbbell uses upper-head support; kettlebell uses horn contacts. Hand pose, wrist posture, implement clearance and attachment points must change with the selected variation.

Basis: Editorial workout-selection relationship supported by each member's direct references; NASM goblet page explicitly permits dumbbell or kettlebell.
