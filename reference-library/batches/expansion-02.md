## Differences that matter

- Front squat: [CrossFit's coaching article](https://www.crossfit.com/pro-coach/developing-the-front-squat) supports the clean-style shoulder rack and explains its preference over crossed-arm variants for transfer to other lifts. StrengthLog permits crossed-arm and strap alternatives. This packet does not combine those grips into one contact preset. The CrossFit images were not inspected.
- Box squat: the publisher describes both a light touch and seated loading. The inspected upright sample does not establish which occurred; the record leaves contact mode unresolved.
- Rack pull: the publisher allows rack or block supports. The image examined shows the loaded plates supported by horizontal plates. The ID therefore identifies a block pull; no rack-pin contact is claimed.
- Safety bar: the sampled implement has a padded yoke and forward handles. Manufacturer, camber dimensions and handle geometry remain unknown; this is distinct from a straight barbell.
- Pin bench press: the upper sample shows safety arms below the bar, while bottom contact comes only from source text. Neither safety height nor bar contact was visually verified.
