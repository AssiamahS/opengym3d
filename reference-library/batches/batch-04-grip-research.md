## Next acquisition: concrete capture brief

Use a coach-reviewed, rights-appropriate demonstration with a consenting performer. This is an evidence request, not an instruction to attempt an unverified load or grip. A coach first identifies whether the vertical shaft candidate is a technique they actually use; if not, record that and leave it unsupported instead of staging it as endorsed technique. Choose equipment and handling conditions with that reviewer.

| Shot | Framing and identity | Acceptance question |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Equipment slate | Whole implement, manufacturer/model, head shape, handle length/diameter, and orientation; measure dimensions separately | Can another reviewer distinguish head support from shaft grip and reproduce the equipment geometry? |
| Vertical shaft candidate, if coach confirms it | Front and both obliques with both complete hands and the exposed shaft visible; stationary hold before any cycle | Are both hands actually on the vertical shaft? Are fingers stacked, interleaved or otherwise arranged? Are palms also bearing on a head? Record each separately. |
| Upper-head dumbbell support | Front, left/right oblique, over each shoulder toward the hands, and low oblique looking toward the underside of the upper head | For each hand, where do palm heel, thumb pad, thumb-index web and each finger meet the head or shaft? Capture a view that exposes each surface; do not infer the hidden side from symmetry. |
| Ordinary bell-down horn grip | Bell body below handle, both hands around handle sides; same bilateral reverse and low-oblique close-ups | Which side of each horn does each thumb occupy? Does the thumb oppose fingers, overlap them, or touch the bell? Is any palm contact on the body present? |
| Separate unloaded explanation | With equipment safely supported, performer points to contact locations, then returns to the demonstrated hold | Does verbal identification match the loaded view? Label explanatory/released-hand poses separately; they cannot substitute for actual grip geometry. |
| Context and continuity | Uncut front and side setup–descent–bottom–return–release, with hands and implement inside frame throughout | Does the same grip persist across phases, or is there a regrip? Match close-ups to the same implement, performer and variation. |

Record original filenames, capture date, exact frame/time locators, camera view, equipment measurements, performer consent and an explicit reuse/derivative license if redistribution is intended. Preserve unknown contact patches as unknown. Have a named human review contact interpretation separately from movement and publication approval. Do not send outreach or upload footage merely on the authority of this brief.
