# Free weight and lower-body reference batch

2026-11-23. **PARTIAL / UNREVIEWED**. Research references only; no motion, anatomy, grip or redistribution approval. No media downloaded. Source text was opened directly. Joint/contact descriptions are qualitative authoring inferences, not measurements. `unknown` camera/phase values are intentional.

Three variations have limited visual inspection: kettlebell horn goblet (standing GIF sample and bottom photo), double dumbbell shoulder squat (standing GIF sample), and dumbbell head-supported goblet (standing photo). Complete playback and dedicated grip close-ups remain absent. The remaining 22 variations have page-text evidence only. Observing a GIF URL does not establish its complete animation or camera view.

The head-supported dumbbell hold and kettlebell horn hold are separate grip IDs. **Dumbbell shaft-held goblet remains RESEARCH REQUIRED** and is not counted as a sourced variation. The [ACE standing reference](https://www.acefitness.org/resources/everyone/exercise-library/362/goblet-squat/) shows upper-head support; [NASM text](https://www.nasm.org/resource-center/exercise-library/goblet-squat) describes cupping the top. Neither verifies a shaft-held goblet technique.

## Complementary-source review

Five priority records now include independently opened publisher text: [ACE bodyweight squat](https://www.acefitness.org/resources/everyone/exercise-library/135/bodyweight-squat/), [NASM horn-hold option](https://www.nasm.org/resource-center/blog/training/how-to-perform-goblet-squats), [NASM side-held Bulgarian split squat, printed page 9](https://trainer.nasm.org/hubfs/NASM_Free%20Guide_Squats%20and%20Deadlifts.pdf?hsLang=en), [NASM dumbbell RDL](https://www.nasm.org/resource-center/exercise-library/dumbbell-romanian-deadlift), and [Horton reverse lunge](https://hortonbarbell.com/reverse-dumbbell-lunge/). These additions do not add visual approval. NASM RDL endpoint differs from the first source; preserve that unresolved distinction. [ACE Bulgarian split squat](https://www.acefitness.org/resources/everyone/exercise-library/366/bulgarian-split-squat/) was excluded as an exact grip match because it uses a chest-held dumbbell.

All 25 records now have explicit setup/action/extreme/return movement phases. These are qualitative text interpretations, separate from observed-media phases. Legacy `movement.end` is preserved; it often means completion of a cycle, not the deepest or overhead pose. Unknown return detail for the clean remains explicit. Counts and approval states are unchanged.
