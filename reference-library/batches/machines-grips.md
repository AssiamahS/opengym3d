## Grip distinctions

[CrossFit's deadlift article](https://www.crossfit.com/essentials/foundational-movement-deadlift-what-is-a-deadlift) distinguishes opposite palm orientations from double overhand and thumb-under-fingers hooking. Its [comparison image](https://www.crossfit.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/04/04111127/GRIPS-1-1024x455.png) was discovered but not visually inspected.

[Catalyst Athletics](https://www.catalystathletics.com/video/1697/Hook-Grip-How-Why/) supplies a useful thumb-web setup sequence and warns against merely crushing the thumb inside a fist. The hook therefore needs separate contact logic. [CrossFit's FAQ](https://www.crossfit.com/faq) defines supinated and neutral orientations. Closed-thumb details remain unresolved where those sources only name palm orientation.

[NASM](https://www.nasm.org/resource-center/exercise-library/goblet-squat) describes a vertical weight cupped at its top. [Muscle & Strength](https://www.muscleandstrength.com/exercises/dumbbell-goblet-squat) gives an under-edge cue in its exercise instructions; a separate staff comment describes two hands on the vertical handle. The latter is weak evidence for a distinct variant, not proof that shaft gripping and head support are interchangeable. Thumb opposition, finger overlap and hand size clearance need close-up review.

The [StrongFirst horns video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2iIKil6wK1E) remains a search-discovered lead because fetching failed. Its grip record is intentionally unresolved. Empty-source placeholder grips must not be mistaken for established technique.
