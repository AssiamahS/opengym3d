## Findings and direct sources

- [Bodyweight step-up](https://www.strengthlog.com/step-up/): Step onto raised support; lower under control.
- [Machine hack squat](https://www.strengthlog.com/hack-squat-machine/): Flex and extend legs against sled.
- [Nordic hamstring eccentric](https://www.strengthlog.com/nordic-hamstring-eccentric/): Knee extension lowers body; arms assist reset.
- [Wall-supported tibialis raise](https://www.strengthlog.com/tibialis-raise/): Ankle dorsiflexion lifts forefeet, then lowers.
- [Kettlebell standing hip flexor raise](https://www.strengthlog.com/standing-hip-flexor-raise/): Hip flexion raises knee and bell; lower.
- [Bodyweight glute bridge](https://www.strengthlog.com/glute-bridge/): Hip extension elevates pelvis; return.
- [Barbell hip thrust](https://www.strengthlog.com/hip-thrust/): Extend hips to lift bar; lower.
- [Machine reverse hyperextension](https://www.strengthlog.com/reverse-hyperextension/): Raise hanging legs toward torso line; lower.
- [Cable hip abduction](https://www.strengthlog.com/cable-machine-hip-abduction/): Move working leg laterally; return.
- [Cable hip adduction](https://www.strengthlog.com/cable-machine-hip-adduction/): Draw attached leg across body; return.
- [Bodyweight clamshell](https://www.strengthlog.com/clamshells/): Open upper bent leg; lower.
- [Bodyweight donkey kick](https://www.strengthlog.com/donkey-kicks/): Raise bent leg behind; lower without grounding.
- [Bodyweight fire hydrant](https://www.strengthlog.com/fire-hydrants/): Lift leg to side; lower.
- [Lateral walk with ankle band](https://www.strengthlog.com/lateral-walk-with-band/): Side steps against band.
- [Reverse Nordic](https://www.strengthlog.com/reverse-nordic/): Lean backward through knees; return.
- [Machine standing calf raise](https://www.strengthlog.com/standing-calf-raise/): Lower heels then plantarflex.
- [Machine seated calf raise](https://www.strengthlog.com/seated-calf-raise/): Lower heels then plantarflex.
- [Bodyweight wall sit](https://uthsc.edu/studentsuccess/campus-rec/documents/wall-sit.pdf): Slide down wall; maintain flexed lower-body hold.
