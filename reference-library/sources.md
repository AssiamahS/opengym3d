## Batch 02 additions and access limits

- [CrossFit dumbbell squat PDF](https://library.crossfit.com/free/pdf/49_06_Dumbbell_Squat.pdf#page=2): inspected frontal standing/bottom pairs for side-held, shoulder-held and two-arm overhead variations; adjacent single-arm image excluded from the two-arm entry.
- [StrongFirst goblet article](https://www.strongfirst.com/kettlebell-simple-sinister-tips-for-heavy-goblet-squats/): Pavel Macek's collage supports coarse horn-junction contact; hidden digits remain unresolved.
- [Fit Women's Weekly](https://fitwomensweekly.com/post/how-to-hold-a-dumbbell-for-goblet-squats/): inspected upper-head support close-up and lowered pose. Generalized pain, load and measured wrist claims excluded.
- [NSCA teaching progressions PDF](https://www.nsca.com/contentassets/3d09f06f0b4c4f6fbd8cc382ed1f3d4a/ptq-10.2.1-progressive-strategies-for-teaching-fundamental-resistance-training-movement-patterns.pdf#page=4): inspected page 4 box-touch and kettlebell-goblet still pairs; no full-motion evidence.
- [E3 Rehab goblet video](https://vimeo.com/832454211): standing poster inspected; player could not play media. Poster-only status retained.
- NASM's embedded video shows kettlebell goblet despite its page's two-implement text. Muscle & Strength's dumbbell video was sampled at explicit timestamps. Neither constitutes complete-cycle review. See [batch review](batches/batch-02-review.md).

[Fit Women's Weekly kettlebell holds](https://fitwomensweekly.com/post/10-ways-to-hold-a-kettlebell/) adds a chest-height photo, not hidden-thumb evidence.

Public accessibility does not grant redistribution rights; source photos/videos remain external, with no media files in the archive.
