## Rebuild and validate

Run `python reference-library/tools/build.py`, then `python reference-library/tools/validate.py`. Inputs are the curated batch JSON plus family/equipment inventory. Generated pages and catalogs are Git-ready research text; downloaded third-party media stays in ignored work/reference-media and is excluded from public bundles.
