# Research method and evidence limits

Research date: 2026-11-23. Repository input: `8ea29c8dab459c306cbc5d20110ffad7584e5417`.
This is internal development evidence, not exercise instruction or approved animation data.

The first collection prioritizes squat, free-weight, upper-body and model-specific machine references. Family inventory entries are editorial research targets, not assertions that technique has been verified. Equipment vocabulary entries describe things to investigate; unspecified dimensions and mechanisms remain unknown. We do not multiply grips, loads, camera angles or tempo labels to manufacture family counts.

Evidence levels are deliberately separate:

- `research_required`: identity or matching reference remains unresolved.
- `partial`: the direct source supports the identity or description, but complementary visual, grip or phase evidence is incomplete.
- `complete`: reserved for checked identity/equipment, complementary inspected visuals, phase/contact descriptions and recorded conflicts. None is promoted merely because a page loads.
- `review_status: unreviewed`: a human has not approved anatomy or movement, regardless of research completeness.

Each reference records its verification depth. A page listing a GIF is not evidence that its pixels, camera, or full repetition were examined. Null view/phase means unknown. A manufacturer product image is not a demonstrated exercise repetition. A direct media URL may change; retain the source page as the durable citation. Source media may be cached for the user-requested internal workflow under ignored work/reference-media; see [local media policy](local-media-policy.md).

`guidance` paraphrases source instruction. `movement` is a qualitative engineering interpretation of that instruction unless the record explicitly identifies visual observation. It is not measured biomechanics. Do not derive exact joint angles, bone coordinates, forces, muscle activation, anthropometry, or safe loading from these records. Visible contact alone does not establish force or pressure.

Source selection favors publishers' own demonstrations and manufacturer documentation. Source reputation does not establish every technique as correct. Review conflicts at the specific variation and model level; do not average incompatible grips. An absent conflict report means conflicts were not systematically resolved, not that consensus exists.

Rights: source media retain their owners' rights; permission to redistribute is unknown unless separately documented. Repository code licensing does not license linked photography. No subscription, restricted API or access-control bypass is used. Local downloads follow the [cache policy](local-media-policy.md). ExRx's robots denial is respected.

Current continuation procedure (user-approved breadth-first scope): choose missing distinct families across categories, usually 1-3 useful variations each. Retain direct source pages and source-discovered photos/videos; inspect when feasible, labeling uninspected links honestly. Record concise setup, action, support, equipment and aliases. Add sensible equipment choices. Fine grips, complementary views and full-cycle gaps do not block breadth; deepen those when selected for animation. Target approximately 300 families; defer the original 1,000-2,000 variations. Validate relationships and update coverage; preserve partial status.

Structural validation checks syntax and referential integrity, not biomechanical truth or future URL availability. Source-link correspondence is checked from retrieved titles and descriptions, with manual visual inspection recorded separately. Every public-use decision still requires a qualified human reviewer.

Batch 04 playback check: a changed player timestamp or seek-slider value alone does not prove that the displayed image has decoded at that time. Inspect a subsequent stable paused screenshot, reject visible buffering/stale poses and record any disagreement. Do not reconstruct a cycle from different takes or silently relabel sampled phase coverage as continuous review. Failed replay should annotate historical evidence without inventing either a successful revalidation or proof that the earlier inspection was false.
