## Bounded engineering experiment

1. **Reproduce the checkpoint.** Start from the current repo, run existing tests and isolate the repair build. Record input/model SHA and preserve old GLB/screenshot evidence. Exit 2 remains an honest PARTIAL.
2. **Diagnose local attachments.** Compare shoulder/clavicle, latissimus/triceps, hip and knee using the same neutral, standing, descent and bottom poses. Inspect bone influences, continuity across neighboring muscle parts, area loss and worst absolute edge changes.
3. **Test at most a small number of explicit candidate fixes.** Prefer attachment-aware constraints, selective existing-joint rotation distribution or portable pose-driven correctives. Keep one factor changed per experiment; preserve original geometry and licenses. A Blender-only modifier is diagnostic unless its effect reaches the exported GLB.
4. **Score both directions.** Measure regional stretch and compression percentiles/counts, triangles approaching zero area, deep self-intersections and visible folds. Do not accept a better maximum stretch if it simply increases collapsed regions.
5. **Export the best *honest* candidate.** Compare browser and Blender posed vertices, annotated front/side/shoulder/hip/knee images, highlighting, loop, foot contact and 320-pixel preview. Keep older artifacts available.
6. **Decision.** Recommend accept for further review, revise with a narrower corrective, or stop and consider a different anatomy/rig. Do not quietly label a partial result PASS.
